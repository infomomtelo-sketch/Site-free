Plain English. No tech knowledge needed.
🏗 Build — Create a website from scratch
1️⃣
Describe your website
Type what you want in plain English. Mention your business name, what it does, sections you need, style and colors. More detail = better result.
Example: "Build a landing page for my coffee shop Brew Lab. Warm dark theme, menu, gallery, location and contact."
2️⃣
Wait 15–20 seconds
AI writes your complete website. You see the animated dots while it works. This uses your Anthropic API credits — about $0.01 per site.
3️⃣
Click ✦ Apply to see it live
When AI finishes, click the orange Apply button at the top. Your website opens in Preview — real, working, in your browser right now.
4️⃣
Download and go live for free
Click Download to save your site. Go to netlify.com/drop, drag the file, get a free live URL in 60 seconds. Or push to your GitHub — Cloudflare auto-deploys it.
Your site costs $0/month to keep live — forever
🔧 Fix Site — Escape a broken builder
1️⃣
Export from your builder first
In Lovable, Wix, Framer or Bolt — look for Export or Download HTML. Save the .html file.
Lovable: Share → Export · Framer: File → Export HTML · Wix: try right-click Save As
2️⃣
Upload it in Fix Site tab
Drag your file onto the drop zone or click Choose file. AI analyzes it immediately.
3️⃣
Download your free clean file
In 15–20 seconds you get a fully cleaned site — no platform scripts, no shutdown threats. Download it and it is yours forever.
